# Privacy Policy for Secret Arcade

**Effective Date:** October 6, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 6, 2026

## 1. Overview
Secret Arcade ("we", "our", or "the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for the Secret Arcade Chrome Extension.

## 2. Information We Collect
**Secret Arcade does NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal browsing data, keystrokes outside the extension, or web traffic.**

The extension operates with strict local-first privacy:
- **Local Game State & High Scores:** Saved strictly on your device using `chrome.storage.local`.
- **User Preferences:** Sound toggle, volume level, theme choice, and disguise screen selection are stored locally on your device.
- **Daily Break Timer:** A local counter tracks play duration (up to 20 minutes daily for free tier) and resets automatically each calendar day.
- **Lifetime License Record:** When you activate a Lifetime Pass, the payment identifier and user-provided email are stored locally in your browser's private extension storage to unlock pro features.

## 3. Remote Data & Network Requests
- The extension runs **100% offline-capable**. Games run completely on your local computer.
- When purchasing a Lifetime Pass, the extension opens the official **Cashfree** checkout page (`cashfree.com`) in a regular browser tab. Cashfree's independent privacy policy governs payment processing.
- No analytics trackers, advertisements, or third-party tracking scripts are bundled into the extension.

## 4. Permissions Used
- **`storage`**: Used exclusively to persist your high scores, audio settings, theme preferences, and lifetime unlock state locally on your machine.

## 5. Data Sharing
We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third parties. All game progress and settings remain in your local browser profile.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Secret Arcade, contact us via our developer support channel or GitHub repository.
